# Northstar University — fictional Planning Policy (2026)

> Demonstration data only. These policies are fictional.

## PLN-CREDIT-LOAD — Recommended semester credits
The planning tool defaults to 12–18 credits per semester. Users can request a different range, but the system must clearly explain when no section combination satisfies constraints.

## PLN-SECTION-CONFLICT — Meeting conflicts
A student cannot select two published sections that overlap on the same weekday. Adjacent times do not count as overlapping. All demo meeting times use the America/New_York timezone.

## PLN-FUTURE-OFFERINGS — Projected courses
When a specific section has not yet been published for a future semester, the platform may display a tentative course sequence based on advertised Fall/Spring offering patterns. These projections are not registration-ready and cannot guarantee actual availability or a conflict-free section schedule.

## PLN-AI-ROLE — AI advisor boundary
The AI assistant may summarize documented academic policies and explain an audit calculated by the rule engine. It cannot override requirements, change a transcript or grade, approve enrollment, or make official graduation decisions.
