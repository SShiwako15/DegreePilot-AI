# Blue Mountain State University — fictional Computer Science Catalog (2026)

> Fictional demonstration data only. Blue Mountain State University and these academic requirements are fictional. Not applicable to a real institution.

## CAT-BSCS-TOTAL — Degree credits
The demonstration BS in Computer Science requires at least 120 unique earned credits, including 44 required core credits, 12 computer-science elective credits, 36 general-education credits, and 28 free-elective credits. A course may contribute to only one requirement category. All listed components must be satisfied.

## CAT-CORE-GRADES — Minimum course grade
A letter grade of C or higher is required for credit in this demonstration catalog. Courses with D, F, W, or I do not earn degree credit. A course repeated with a subsequent passing grade can contribute credit once. Pass grades may count only in explicitly authorized elective categories.

## CAT-PREREQUISITES — Catalog prerequisite rules
Prerequisites are evaluated from each course's prerequisite groups in the student's pinned catalog. Every group must be satisfied; within a group, completing any one listed course is sufficient. A prerequisite must be completed with a letter grade of C or better before the target term; taking it in the same term does not satisfy the requirement. A course with no prerequisite groups has no course prerequisites. Eligibility also depends on term offerings and, for published sections, available seats. Unpublished future offerings are tentative and cannot guarantee seats or a conflict-free timetable.

## CAT-PREREQ-CS301 — Algorithms eligibility
CS301 Algorithms requires successful completion of CS201 Data Structures and at least one of MATH250 Discrete Mathematics or STAT201 Statistics before the term in which CS301 is taken.

## CAT-PREREQ-CS350 — Computer Networks eligibility
CS350 Computer Networks requires successful completion of CS201 Data Structures before the term in which CS350 is taken.

## CAT-PREREQ-CS401 — Capstone eligibility
CS401 Senior Capstone requires successful completion of both CS301 Algorithms and CS310 Operating Systems before the term in which CS401 is taken.

## CAT-GRADUATION — Graduation audits
Degree completion requires all required core courses, each credit category minimum, and at least 120 distinct earned credits under the student's assigned published catalog version. A generated audit is informational and not an official university graduation determination.
